THE VISION BEHIND FGF

Meet Our Founder

The passion, knowledge, and vision behind Feel Good Fitness.

FOUNDER & CEO

Dr. Payel Banerjee

Dr. Payel Banerjee is a distinguished fitness specialist and industry leader with over 15 years of experience in health, fitness, and performance training.

As the Founder & CEO of Feel Good Fitness, she combines evidence-based exercise science, functional movement, and personalized training to help individuals build sustainable strength, confidence, and lifelong well-being.

Honored with an Honorary Doctorate in Fitness, Dr. Payel is passionate about making fitness accessible to everyone—not just bodybuilders or elite athletes.

Areas of Expertise

Weight Loss & Body Composition Sports Nutrition Strength & Conditioning HIIT Functional Training Pilates & Movement Therapy
OUR FOUNDER’S VISION
“Fitness is not just a temporary goal or simply about lifting heavy weights—it is an essential pillar of everyday life. My mission is to break the myth that gym training is only for bodybuilders or elite athletes. Strength, movement, and structured physical activity belong in everyone’s daily routine, especially women, who often need strength training for long-term health and vitality. Fitness is not just a sport; it is a fundamental lifestyle choice that empowers every individual to live stronger, healthier, and better.”
